Join the Summer Reading Leaderboard by taking Accelerated Reader quizzes.

Use the steps below to get started and earn points for your summer goal.

How to log in to Accelerated Reader (AR) for the first time

  • Go to Westminster’s Login Page.
  • Click the button that says “I am a Student.”
  • Enter your username and password.
  • Click Log In.

How to Find and Take a Quiz

  • On your student homepage, click the orange Accelerated Reader button.
  • In the search bar, type the title of the book or the author’s name, then click Search.
  • When you see your book pop up, click Take Quiz.
  • Choose “I read it by myself” (or “Someone read to me”), then select Start Quiz.

Tip for Success

Take the quiz right after finishing the book while the story is still fresh in your mind. Once you pass, your points will instantly add to your summer goal.
